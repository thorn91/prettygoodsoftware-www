Tally · a calorie tracker for iPhone

As quick as the AI apps. As accurate as the search ones.

Write a meal the way you would say it, like “half a turkey sandwich and a cup of tomato soup”. Tally looks up every food in real nutrition databases and logs the calories. There is no food list to scroll and nothing to weigh.

iPhone · iOS 17+ · TestFlight beta

Why Tally

Other calorie apps make you pick one.

Search-and-pick trackers

Accurate, and a chore

Every food on the plate is its own search, a pick from a long list, and a portion to enter. It works if you keep up with it.

Snap-a-photo apps

Quick, and a guess

An AI looks at the meal and estimates everything, calories included. You can’t tell where a number came from.

Tally

Quick, and from real data

You type the meal in a sentence and Tally does the looking up. Calories come from real nutrition databases. When Tally has to guess or estimate, it tells you, and asks if the guess matters.

Easy to log. Accurate where it counts.

3 shots, swipe

  1. Easy

    One sentence per meal

    Type it the way you would say it, or paste a recipe link. No searching, no scrolling, no scale.

  2. Accurate

    Every food matched to real nutrition data

    Each food on the plate gets its own calories from a nutrition database. When Tally has to estimate, it tells you.

  3. Easy and accurate

    Only check what could matter

    When a portion is a guess big enough to move your day, Tally asks. Everything else is done.

Packaged food

Scan it instead.

Point the camera at the barcode. Tally finds the product and shows the serving and calories from its package label. One tap logs it.

No barcode handy? Type the numbers under the bars. Not in any database? Describe it in words like any other meal.

Your day and your week

Today shows the day’s calories as a ring, one slice per meal, and what is left.

Week adds up the last seven days, so a big dinner and a light lunch the next day even out. Protein, fat, carbs and sodium sit under both.

Companion

Tally the abacus, if you want it

Tally the abacus is an optional companion. The app offers it once, and Show Tally in Settings turns it off.

On the Week tab it notes how many days you logged.

Where it shows up

  • Empty day

    Says hello on a day with nothing logged.

  • Meal lookup

    Counts beads while a meal is looked up.

  • Review, last food

    Cheers when the last food in Review is settled.

  • Review, caught up

    Rests when Review is caught up.

Also in Tally

  • Paste a recipe link and Tally reads the recipe from the page.
  • Write “serves 4” and Tally logs your share.
  • A meal you have logged before comes back in one tap.
  • Fix a meal in words: “less rice than that”.
  • Forgot a food? Search for it and add it.
